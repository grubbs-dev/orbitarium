# Code of Conduct

Orbitarium is a small project, and small projects work best when people make room for curiosity, clarity, and kindness.

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different experience levels. Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not welcome.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or decline contributions that do not meet this standard.
